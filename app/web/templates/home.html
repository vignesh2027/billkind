Why bills, and why the biller

The gap is small

37% of US adults would not cover a 400 USD emergency with cash. One in six US households is behind on utility bills. A 60 USD power bill decides whether the lights stay on.

Federal Reserve SHED 2026; NEADA June 2026

Crowdfunding misses it

In a study of 437,596 medical fundraisers only 12% reached their goal and 16% got nothing. A single small bill never gets a campaign at all.

American Journal of Public Health, 2022

Givers want certainty

Two thirds of donors say trust is essential, yet fewer than one in five highly trust charities. Paying a verified biller directly means you know exactly what your money did.

Give.org Donor Trust Report 2026

How it works

Snap the bill

AI reads the photo or PDF, fills in the biller, amount and due date, and masks personal details.

Nine checks

Verified biller, edit traces, two independent readings, duplicates, velocity and more. Risky bills go to a person.

Swipe and pay

Sponsors pay all or part with PayPal, in the bill's currency. Several sponsors can pool into one bill.

Biller paid, live

One PayPal payout to the biller's verified account, a receipt, a PAID stamp and a thank-you, in real time.
