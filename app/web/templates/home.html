How it works

Three steps. One PAID stamp.

You don't have to tell your whole story or ask friends for money. Post the bill and we'll tell you when it's paid.

  1. 01

    Drop a bill

    Snap a photo or drop a PDF. Billkind reads the biller, amount, due date and account number for you, and shows how sure it is about each one.

  2. 02

    A stranger pays with PayPal

    Sponsors swipe through verified bills and cover some or all of one with PayPal, in the bill's own currency.

  3. 03

    The biller gets paid

    Once the bill is covered, a PayPal payout goes to the biller with the account number to credit. The PAID stamp shows up live.

The one rule

Only the biller gets paid.

We only send money to billers on our verified list, like schools, clinics, utilities and landlords. If a poster lies, the worst that happens is a real bill gets paid.

Giving agent

Set your rules once. Your giving agent pays by them.

Write what you want to support in plain words, like "up to $100 a month for school fees, never more than $40 per bill." The agent turns that into limits you confirm, then pays matching bills with your saved PayPal and writes down why.

  • You set the limits. Your budget, per-bill cap, bill types and risk limit are checked in code before any money moves.
  • Pays while you're away. PayPal Vault saves your PayPal once, so the agent can pay inside your limits. Stop it any time.
  • Every call is logged. Each payment, skip and block shows up in the decision log.
