How it works

Three steps from an unpaid bill to a PAID stamp.

No campaign to write, no network to ask, no money handed to a stranger. A bill goes in, a verified biller gets paid, and everyone can see it happen.

Step 1

Post the bill

Take a photo or upload the PDF. AI reads the biller, amount, due date and account number, and hides names, phone numbers and addresses.

Checked, then listed

{{ ui('shield') }} The one rule

Pay the biller. Never the person.

Money only moves to a biller whose PayPal account a person has verified: the school, the clinic, the utility, the landlord. The worst a dishonest poster could do is get someone's real bill paid. That removes the reason to lie.
