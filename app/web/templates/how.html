How Billkind works

A joke about strangers paying your bills, made safe.

Sponsors pay the biller directly, so there is nothing to steal and nothing to misuse. Here is every rule that makes that true.

The one ruleNine checksPrivacyRefundsSandbox
{{ ui('shield') }} The one rule

Money only moves to a verified biller.

Money only moves to a verified biller's PayPal account: the school, clinic, utility or landlord on the bill. It never goes to the person who posted. The worst a dishonest poster could do is get someone's real bill paid.

Fraud checks

Nine checks on every bill.

Low-risk bills go live automatically. Anything else waits for a person. Hard failures, like a bill that was already paid, are refused with the reason shown.

  1. The biller is in our verified registry, with a PayPal account confirmed by a person.
  2. The file is not saved by photo or document editing software.
  3. The image shows no visual signs of editing.
  4. Two independent readings (an AI model and plain text recognition) agree on the amount and date.
  5. Line items add up to the total.
  6. The amount is typical for that kind of bill.
  7. The due date makes sense.
  8. It is not a duplicate, by file, by image similarity, or by account and amount.
  9. The poster is not posting too many bills or too much money at once.
{{ ui('eye-off') }} Privacy

Sponsors see the bill, not the person.

What sponsors see

The kind of bill, the city, the amount, the due date, the last four characters of the account number, and a short note written from the poster's own words with their consent.

What nobody sees

Names, phone numbers, email addresses and street addresses are removed. Only the biller receives the full account number, inside the PayPal payout note.

{{ ui('refund') }} Refunds

If a bill cannot be paid, you get your money back.

If a bill is withdrawn, reaches its due date before it is covered, cannot be paid out to the biller, or another sponsor covers it first, Billkind refunds you through PayPal automatically. You can see each refund on your giving page.

Sandbox only

This is a hackathon build. Every payment runs in the PayPal sandbox and no real money moves.
